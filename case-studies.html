Case Studies

A closer look at selected projects.

01

Huddle Workspace

Problem

The task was to turn a product design brief into a usable team collaboration interface with authentication, workspace navigation, channel views, and messaging states.

My Role

I focused on frontend implementation, user flow, screen structure, interaction states, and how users move through the workspace experience.

What I Built

I worked around registration and login screens, workspace structure, channel pages, messaging layouts, loading states, empty states, and user interaction patterns.

What I Learned

I learned how important it is to break a product into smaller flows, follow designs closely, communicate with teammates, and still keep the interface practical.

02

Todo App Assessment

Problem

The application needed to meet specific exam requirements, including listing, search, filtering, pagination, detail pages, error handling, and accessibility expectations.

My Role

I reviewed the app against requirements, identified weak areas, and worked through improvements with attention to behaviour and compliance.

What I Improved

The work involved pagination logic, search and status filtering, detail route review, custom 404 behaviour, labels, focus behaviour, and live region usage.

What It Shows

This project shows my ability to read requirements carefully, inspect an existing implementation, verify behaviour, and improve a project without adding unrelated features.

03

CodeAlpha Calculator

Problem

The task required a functional calculator using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with clear operations, responsive layout, and a polished user experience.

My Role

I designed the interface, structured menu navigation, and built the logic for basic calculation, scientific mode, conversion tools, notes, and history.

What I Added

I added basic and scientific modes, currency and unit conversion, math notes, calculation history, keyboard support, mobile layout adjustments, and sign toggle.

What It Shows

This project shows my ability to go beyond a basic requirement while keeping the app organized, usable, and suitable for a frontend internship submission.
