Quick Profile

I build frontend projects that turn ideas into polished, usable web experiences.

I am a frontend developer focused on building responsive websites, clean layouts, and practical user interfaces. I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Vue, Git, and GitHub to turn project ideas into working digital experiences that people can actually use.

My frontend work is shaped by clear structure, mobile responsiveness, readable content, and careful interaction design. I pay attention to how pages are arranged, how buttons behave, how content scales across screen sizes, and how users move from one action to another without confusion.

I also bring a research background from Criminology and Security Studies, which helps me think carefully about people, behaviour, trust, and decision making. This gives my frontend work a stronger user focused direction because I do not only design for appearance, I design for clarity and confidence.

Alongside development, I have experience in academic writing and AI quality review. I write research projects, IT reports, seminar papers, and Individualized Directed Study work, and I also evaluate AI responses for accuracy, instruction following, helpfulness, and natural communication. These skills support my frontend work by helping me communicate clearly, organize content properly, and review details carefully.
