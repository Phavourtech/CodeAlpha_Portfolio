Quick Profile

A mix of frontend skill, criminology thinking, academic writing, and quality review.

I am Ugochukwu Favour, a frontend developer with a background in Criminology and Security Studies. My work brings together design, logic, research, and human behaviour. I enjoy building web interfaces that are clear, responsive, and easy to use, while also paying attention to how people think, make decisions, and move through digital products.

I like work that needs structure. Whether I am building an interface, reviewing AI output, or writing academic research, I care about clarity, instruction following, and the final user experience.

Through my frontend training with AltSchool Africa, I have been improving my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Vue, Git, GitHub, responsive design, DOM manipulation, routing, and component based development. I care about layouts that feel clean, buttons that behave properly, pages that work well on mobile, and user experiences that do not confuse the person using them.

My criminology background has shaped the way I think about people, systems, safety, behaviour, and investigation. It helps me approach problems with patience and attention to detail. I do not only look at what a page does, I also think about why a user may hesitate, where they may get confused, and how a better structure can make the experience feel more trustworthy.

I am also an academic writer. I write final year research projects, IT reports, seminar papers, and Individualized Directed Study work. This has strengthened my ability to research carefully, organize information, explain ideas clearly, and present work in a structured way. That writing experience also helps me communicate better when planning projects, documenting ideas, and turning complex information into something people can understand.

I am building experience in AI evaluation and quality review as well. I review responses, compare outputs, check instructions, and look closely at whether digital content is accurate, helpful, natural, and easy to trust. Across all my work, I try to combine technical skill, research discipline, careful writing, and a strong understanding of people.
