Profile

My background helps me notice what others may skip.

01

Frontend Development

I build responsive web interfaces using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and Vue. My frontend work is not only about making a page look presentable. I care about how a user understands a screen, where their attention goes first, what happens when they click a button, and whether the layout still feels natural on a phone.

Through my AltSchool Africa training and personal projects, I have worked on image galleries, calculators, todo applications, and collaborative workspace interfaces. These projects helped me practise form validation, routing, reusable components, filtering, pagination, lightbox views, keyboard support, error states, loading states, and mobile responsive layouts.

02

Criminology

My criminology training shaped the way I think. It taught me to observe patterns, ask better questions, study human behaviour, and look beyond the surface of a problem. That background helps me approach digital products with patience and context.

03

Academic Writing

I write and support final year research papers, IT reports, seminar projects, and Individualized Directed Study projects. This has strengthened my research organization, chapter structuring, literature review writing, referencing, editing, and academic presentation.

04

AI Quality Work

I evaluate AI responses, review documents, and check content against detailed instructions. This work has strengthened my accuracy, consistency, and ability to follow requirements carefully.
